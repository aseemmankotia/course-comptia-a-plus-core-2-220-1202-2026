# Chapter 10 Lab: Backup Strategies, Safety Procedures, and Environmental Controls

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Design a backup schedule for a small office applying the 3-2-1 rule specifying full/incremental backup days, then complete an ESD-safe component swap procedure checklist

## Key takeaway

Apply the 3-2-1 backup rule literally, including the offsite requirement, and know the restore-time tradeoffs between backup types before choosing one for a scenario.
