## Chapter 12: Full Exam Simulation and Test-Taking Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | 220-1202 exam: total questions and total time allowed? | Up to 90 questions in 90 minutes. Exam tip: budget ~60 sec/question average, with extra reserved upfront for PBQs. |
| 2 | 220-1202 passing score and scale? | 700 out of a 100-900 scaled score range. Exam tip: the scaled score, not raw question count, determines pass/fail. |
| 3 | Where do PBQs typically appear in the exam sequence? | Near the beginning of the exam. Exam tip: budget a larger upfront time block (e.g. first 10-15 min) for PBQs before starting multiple-choice questions. |
| 4 | Test center vs online-proctored: key environment difference? | Online-proctored requires a live webcam room scan and a completely clear desk before starting; test center provides a controlled room by default. Exam tip: neither format allows notes or scratch paper unless provided by the proctor. |
| 5 | How to recognize a multiple-response question? | Look for explicit instruction text like 'select two' or 'choose all that apply.' Exam tip: selecting the wrong number of options marks the entire question wrong — no partial credit. |
| 6 | PBQ types on the 220-1202 exam? | Drag-and-drop (matching/ordering) and simulation (interacting with a mock interface/terminal). Exam tip: simulations take longer — budget more time per simulation PBQ than per drag-and-drop PBQ. |
| 7 | Flag-and-review workflow: when to use it? | Flag any question where you're unsure after process of elimination, submit your best guess, then move on immediately. Exam tip: never leave a question fully blank — blank answers are always scored wrong. |
| 8 | Process-of-elimination strategy for scenario questions? | Eliminate clearly unrelated/wrong options first, then match remaining plausible options against specific scenario details before choosing. Exam tip: use this before flagging, not instead of flagging. |
| 9 | Average time budget per standard multiple-choice question? | ~60 seconds. Exam tip: this average assumes PBQ time is budgeted separately upfront, not drawn from this per-question average. |
| 10 | What does the domain-level score report show on a failed attempt? | Relative performance strength/weakness by exam domain — not specific missed questions. Exam tip: use it to prioritize retake study, since actual exam questions are never disclosed. |
| 11 | Should a candidate spend unlimited time perfecting one PBQ? | No. PBQs are not weighted higher than multiple-choice questions. Exam tip: if a PBQ stalls badly, submit a best attempt, flag it, and preserve time for remaining questions. |
| 12 | Online-proctored exam workspace rule? | Desk/workspace must be completely clear — no papers, notes, second monitors, or phones. Exam tip: violations can pause or terminate the exam session. |
