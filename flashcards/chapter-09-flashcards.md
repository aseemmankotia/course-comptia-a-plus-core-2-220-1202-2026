## Chapter 9: Documentation, Ticketing, and Change Management — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | What are the 4 core fields every support ticket must document? | Issue description, category, priority, resolution. Exam tip: missing any of these makes a ticket 'incomplete' on scenario questions. |
| 2 | Change management: what document tells you HOW to undo a change if it fails? | The rollback plan. Exam tip: rollback plan is the #1 tested 'missing element' answer on change management scenario questions. |
| 3 | Change management: what step happens BEFORE requesting approval, to validate the change won't break production? | Sandbox testing. Exam tip: sandbox = isolated test environment, separate from production. |
| 4 | What is the correct order of a change request: purpose, scope, risk analysis, sandbox testing, approval? | Purpose → Scope → Risk analysis → Sandbox testing → Approval → Implementation. Exam tip: approval always comes right before implementation, never first. |
| 5 | What is a Knowledge Base (KB) article used for vs. a change request? | KB article = documented steps for a routine known task/SOP. Change request = proposal to modify a production system requiring approval. Exam tip: password reset steps = KB/SOP, not a change request. |
| 6 | What must be assigned to hardware immediately upon receipt in asset management? | An asset tag, and the device must be entered into the inventory list. Exam tip: tagging happens at intake, before deployment to users. |
| 7 | What two dates in asset management help determine if a device should be replaced? | Purchase date and warranty expiration date. Exam tip: expired warranty + old purchase date = flag for replacement in procurement lifecycle. |
| 8 | What are the stages of the procurement/asset lifecycle? | Request → Purchase → Receiving/Tagging → Deployment → Maintenance → Retirement/Disposal. Exam tip: disposal must follow data destruction/e-waste procedures. |
| 9 | EULA vs. Open Source license — key difference? | EULA (commercial) restricts use/copying/source access to licensed terms. Open source permits viewing, modifying, and redistributing source code. Exam tip: GPL/MIT = open source; Windows license = EULA. |
| 10 | What is DRM (Digital Rights Management) used for? | Restricts copying/use of licensed digital content (software, media) to enforce licensing terms. Exam tip: DRM prevents unauthorized duplication, not the same as encryption. |
| 11 | What does the 'risk analysis' step in change management determine? | The potential impact and likelihood of problems if the change is implemented — used to decide if the change proceeds and what mitigation (rollback) is needed. Exam tip: high risk changes require more thorough sandbox testing and stakeholder approval. |
| 12 | Who typically must approve a change before implementation? | A change advisory board (CAB) or designated approver, per company policy. Exam tip: technician cannot self-approve their own production change, regardless of perceived risk level. |
